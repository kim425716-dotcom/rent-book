⚙️ Rent Review Notice Customizer

How Balances & Arrears Work

1. Automatic Monthly Charges

Rent automatically accrues each month starting from the tenant's Move-in Month up to the selected viewing month. Vacant units do not accrue charges.

2. Monthly Utility Bills

Bills you add (such as Water, Electricity, Garbage, or Repairs) are billed to the specific month you specify, adding to that month's total charges.

3. Arrears Carried Forward

If a tenant did not clear their balance in previous months, the unpaid remainder carries forward into the current month as the Opening Balance (Arrears B/F).

4. Part Payments & Overpayments (Credit)

When a tenant pays:

  • Part payment: The outstanding balance reduces, showing the remaining unpaid portion.
  • Overpayment: If a tenant pays more than they owe, the excess is stored as a Credit balance, automatically deducting from their next month's rent!

5. Viewing Different Months

Use the month selector at the top (e.g. ‹ October 2026 ›) at any time to audit previous months or view future expected rent.
